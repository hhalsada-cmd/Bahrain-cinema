Experience

The kid's cinema at EPIX will have added attractions with a soft play area and slides incorporated into the cinema hall. Kids can enjoy the cinema while playing as well as they can relax on the comfortable lounger or recliner. There is dedicated seating for the adults too.

The XL theatre are our large format theaters offering the ultimate cinema experience. It can accommodate 125 to 200 guests. The XL theaters offers four types of seating for the guest to select and enjoy their cinema. XL offers the latest technology such as Dolby Atmos and Christie 4K Laser Projection.

The XL Balcony theatre offers the ultimate cinema experience. With two layers of seating, it can accommodate 298 guests and balcony offers four types of seating for the guest to select and enjoy their cinema. The latest technology such as Dolby Atmos and Christie 4K Laser Projection will surround you.

The VIP Cinema will pamper the guest with its fully reclining seats and the dedicated lounge with well trained staff and handpicked menu will make your movie experience unique.

COMPANY

Epix Cinemas is fully owned subsidiary of Dadabai Holding operating at Dana Mall, Bahrain. It boasts 10 screens, with a full capacity of over 1100 seats.
